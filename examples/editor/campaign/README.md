# Station Routes

A small, original campaign example with a typewriter introduction, a route choice,
a training level and an ending passage. The training artwork is the project's
redistributable workshop art. Walk to the far right of its final room to finish.
No Flashback assets are included; provide your own extracted assets to play.

Run `./edit-local.sh --campaign-editor` for a private editable copy. Select stages,
connect OUT to IN, edit exit names, add movies from your asset catalog, or open the
story layout window to place text and photos. See `docs/CAMPAIGN_EDITOR.md`.
