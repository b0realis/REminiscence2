# REminiscence2 credits

Delphine Software created Flashback, released in 1992. REminiscence2 exists
out of gratitude for that work and the people who have kept it accessible.

- **Gregory Montoir (cyx)** created REminiscence and continued developing its
  engine and platform support.
- **Alex Chermenin** developed the REinforced fork used as this project's
  starting point. His contributions also appear in the upstream history as
  Alexander and Aleksandr Chermenin.
- **Jonathan Bidwell, PhD** contributed the 2026 room and entity guards,
  demo behavior fixes, and tracing present in the imported tree.
- **Yaz0r, Pixel, and gawd** shared information about the game, as acknowledged
  in the original README.
- **Zenju** authored the inherited xBRZ scaler code.
- **Sean Barrett and stb contributors** provided the inherited Vorbis decoder
  and the editor's image decoder.
- **Niels Lohmann and JSON for Modern C++ contributors** provided the editor's
  JSON parser and serializer.
- **Nuke.YKT** authored Nuked OPL3, used by the new AdLib music backend.
- **FluidSynth and Munt contributors** provide the optional software synthesizers.
- **SDL, zlib, and libmodplug contributors**, port maintainers, translators,
  reverse engineers, and players made this work possible.

The original copyright and license notices remain with the source.
[Upstream history](https://github.com/chermenin/REminiscence/commits/2200e18c6e5eb710b05c5095a751882591c21cf8/)
records the imported contributions. REminiscence2 starts its own branch with a
single root commit. Please keep these acknowledgments in
future distributions and extend them as contributors join.

REminiscence2 is an independent community project. Credit expresses gratitude;
it does not imply endorsement by the original creators or rights holders.
