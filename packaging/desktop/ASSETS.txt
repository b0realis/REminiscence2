Copy your original, prepared Flashback game files into this folder.
Keep editions in separate subfolders (for example amiga/, gog/ and mac/).
Subdirectories are searched automatically. A picker remembers your edition;
QoL / Session lets you change it. --list-assets prints available IDs.
Optional Ogg soundtracks go in music/<pack-name>/, selected in QoL / Audio.
See manual.md for track names and supported prepared soundtrack formats.
Amiga WHDLoad .lha archives must first be prepared with tools/extract_amiga.py.
DOS/GOG: copy the original DOS resource files, not the installer/executable.
Macintosh: provide FLASHBACK.BIN or FLASHBACK.RSRC preserving resource data.
REminiscence2 includes the engine only; no commercial game files are included.
Saves and preferences use your writable user-data folder by default.
See manual.md and RELEASES.md in the package for details.
